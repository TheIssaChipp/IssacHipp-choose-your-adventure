# Life of Peril

 # The years pass by...

 You're cellmate makes you do their laundry and nobody at the prison respects you. Even the line cooks have given you smaller portions now.

Until one day, a guard knocks on your cell, the metal clanging against their baton.

"You have a visitor", they grunt out.

They cuff you and take you to the visitors area. On the opposite side of the bulletproof glass sits a man who looks faintly familiar.

You sit and pull the phone of the hook.

You greet them, "Hello, who are you?"

"Who am I?", they say.
"I'm the officer who arrested you years ago, in that pizza parlor. That watch of yours, when I confiscated it, sent me down a spiral... And ruined my life".

You're stunned.
