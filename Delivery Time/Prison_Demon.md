# Prison Demon

You gear up your right hand, and clock them right against the chin. Your hand aches and they look at you, very confused.

"What the heck was that for?", they ask.

You stretch out your hand, which aches with a dull pain, and shrug.

They stand and grab your head, and push it into the toilet, giving you a swirlie.

The prisoners that see you laugh at your failure.
