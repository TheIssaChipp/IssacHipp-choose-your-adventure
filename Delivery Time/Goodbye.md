# Goodbye

You take the watch to the nearest pawn shop.

When you walk in, it smells like cigarrettes.

You go to the counter, and place the watch on the glass top.

The worker there puts on some gloves and analyzes the watch.

"I'll give you fifty bucks for it", they say.

"What? Come on, at least 200?", you respond.

"Can't do it. I'm taking all the risk with this thing. Who knows if its real gold on here. Fifty, take it or leave it".
