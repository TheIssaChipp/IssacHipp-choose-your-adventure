# Homeless

You think back to your apartment as you roam the streets. Sleeping on boxes has worn on your spine.

One cold night, while sleeping on a bench, a newspaper blows by and smacks your face. You open the paper to a "Now Hiring" page.

Only one job is there. A pizza delivery job.
