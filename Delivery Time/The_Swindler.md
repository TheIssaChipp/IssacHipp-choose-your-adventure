# The Swindler

You sit straight up, and put on your best authoritative voice.

"If you get me out of here, I'll tell you everything".

The man's eyes open wide, then he nods.

"Okay. Hold on".

The man stands, and calls over a prison guard. His mouth moves, but you can't hear through the glass and since he isn't talking into the phone.

Minutes later, you're given your belongings including the watch, which you fasten on your wrist. The man stands beside you the entire time.

"Cops can just get people out of prison?", you ask.

"If you have the clearance level I have- well, had... Then you can".

He brings you to the front of the prison, and immediately gets to barraging you with questions.

"Where'd you get it?"..."Is that real gold?"..."Is it cursed?"..."Why'd you punch that pizza parlor boss?"...
