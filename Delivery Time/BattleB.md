# Battle

You jump over the fence with the pizzas. A slight shift in their boxes weight almost slips them out of your hands, but you recover.

But as you recover, you hear the click-clack of unclipped dog nails strike against the concrete walkway coming from the backyard of the house.

As the dog steps into the walkway, you and it make eye contact. A mexican standoff, so to speak.

Neither one of you moves.
