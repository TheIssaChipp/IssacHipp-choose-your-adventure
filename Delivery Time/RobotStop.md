# Robot Stop

You push past the crowd, strap the pizzas to the back of the bike, and zip through the streets as fast as you can.

Through stop signs and crosswalks, you reach the street you needed.

When you check the time, you see you only have five minutes until the delivery is over thirty minutes.

When you finally reach the house, there is a sign on the front of the house, which reads "Beware of dogs". The house is encircled by a chain link fence.
