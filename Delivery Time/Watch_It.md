# Watch It!

You kick the door open, knocking it off its hinges, and stepping inside.

As the wooden door clanks against the ground, you hear a yelp in the backyard of the house, and a moment later, the homeowner steps in through a patio door, holding a chair in one hand and a phone with 911 already on the line.

"Who are you!?", they shout.

"I'm here with the pizza!", you exclaim.

"I didn't order a pizza, get out of my house! The police are around the corner!", they yell.

You back out of the house, but the cops are already there, and throw you in cuffs before you can take your hands off the pizza.

Your watch digs into your wrist, just above the cuffs.
