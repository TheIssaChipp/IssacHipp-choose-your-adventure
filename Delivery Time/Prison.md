# Prison

You have swapped out your pizza parlor uniform for an orange jumpsuit, and your prized watch is sitting in a locker somewhere out of your reach.

As you walk into your prison cell, you're met with your cellmate. A seven foot tall bodybuilder sits on their bed, staring you down as the guards push you in and lock the cell.

All the movie quotes ring in your head, "When you go to prison... Punch the biggest guy there, everyone will respect you".
