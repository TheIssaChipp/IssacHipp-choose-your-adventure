# The Inevitable

Before you can do anything, the former officer grabs both your hands and puts them behind your back.

"No you don't!"

"This is what you get, for your dishonesty".

He calls the prison, and they pick you up soon after. Before they take you away, he unclasps the watch from your wrist and pockets it.

"I don't need your answers anyway", he says.

Nobody listens to you yelling about your watch.
