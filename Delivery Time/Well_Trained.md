## Ending 5: The Boring One
# Well Trained

You apologize for the interuption and trespassing, and bike back to the pizza parlor.

When you arrive, your boss looks at you, indifferent.

"As you can see, there was an error with the writing. However, we commend your proper due-diligence and are giving you a promotion to Head Pizza Director of Operations here at our pizza parlor. Congratulations", she mutters.

Immediately, a trainer ushers you into the back of the store, and into an office with no windows and a torn up office chair.

"Congratulations on your promotion! Here is your new office!" the trainer says cheerfully.

"What do I do now?" You ask.

"Well, you're the Head Pizza Director of Operations now, you can do whatever you want".

"But all I did was my job? Everyone else is too", you question.

"Yes, but the boss found you rather easy to control, so she put you in a powerful position".

"Am I paid a livable wage?", you ask.

"Barely!", the trainer hollers before shutting your door and leaving you in the office.

The ceiling fan whirs, causing a slight hum in the room.

You feel incomplete.
