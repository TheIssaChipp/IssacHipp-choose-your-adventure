## Ending Three: Home Is Where The Heart Is

You spend your life roaming the streets from town to town. Sleeping in the trees in the winter and camping on the mountains in the summer.

While the weather is sometimes harsh, you enjoy exploring the world.

You look at your watch often, now covered in dirt and rust. Sometimes you think about what you did wrong.

But as you watch the sunset on the side of a mountain, sharp shadows from the trees, you feel at peace, and toss the watch over the edge.
