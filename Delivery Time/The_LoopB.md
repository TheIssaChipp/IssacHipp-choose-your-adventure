## Ending Four: The Loop Ends.
# Isn't This Where We Came In?

You work for weeks. The boss doesn't seem to like you, or notice you, or care about you... They only need fast delivery. Since introducing a new "Thirty minutes or less" delivery deal, you're pay has been docked for going over numerous times, and frustration is rising for you, fast. So much frustration, that you forgot your bike lock at home today.

Tonight you have the night shift. It is 10pm when you arrive at work and get a delivery instantly pushed into your hands. Your only device of delivery - besides on foot - is a store provided bike. You pedal as hard as you can there, and back to the parlor.

You work in the parlor for a long time. You're never completely broke, but never comfortable.

You wish you never sold your watch for a mere fifty bucks, especially when rent is hundreds of dollars.

You don't ever feel satisfied.
