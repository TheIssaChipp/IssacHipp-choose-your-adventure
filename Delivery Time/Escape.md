# Escape

That night, Kelly wakes you up in the middle of the night. The prison is pitch black.

A dark figure at the cell entrance lets you out, before skedaddling away.

You and a few other innmates are sneaking over the fence in the yard, when suddenly lights beam onto your crew.

Yelling ensues, and guards start shouting.

Kelly boosts you over the fence and as you land on the grass on the other side, you watch as they gets taken away by the guards.

"Remember me!" they shout.

You and the few innmates that got over the fence scatter and run into the night.
