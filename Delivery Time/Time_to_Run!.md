# Time to Run!

You sprint as fast as you can away from the prison. You're head spins around for a bus, train, taxi, anything!

While running, you pass a familiar pizza parlor. Your eyes gaze inside and for a second, your attention is off of the footchase. You notice there is no "30 minutes or less!" delivery sign.

Until...

You realize the sign has slipped off it's stand, and is under your shoe. As you push off that foot the sign slips away, and throws your balance off. You tumble face first into the ground, and the cop catchs up with you. You can't see him, but you hear his footsteps. Your gold watch glistens on your wrist.
