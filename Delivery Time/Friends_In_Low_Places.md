# Friends In Low Places

"I like you're jumpsuit. The orange suits you!", you say.

They look at you at first confused, then they smile.

"I like your jumpsuit too", they respond.

"Wanna' be friends?", you ask.

"Okay!", they say.

# Years pass...

The two of you are a match made in heaven. Kelly, you found out, was their name.

You both lifted together in the yard, and in the winter played "Old Maid" at night in your cell.

Eventually, Kelly comes to you with an idea.

"Hey, I found some contacts outside, they're gonna' break us out of jail. You in?"
